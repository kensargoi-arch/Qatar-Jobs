Qatar Work Visa - Documents You Will Need

These are the standard documents Qatari employers and the Ministry of Labour typically request. Have these ready or in progress before you apply.

  • Valid passport (at least 6 months validity)
  • Recent passport-size photo, white background
  • Signed employment contract / job offer
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • Police clearance certificate
  • Educational or trade certificate (if applicable to the role)
  • Valid driving licence (for driver/rider roles)
  • Biometric / fingerprint appointment (arranged after offer)
Under Qatar labour law, your employer or sponsor covers visa and recruitment costs. You should never be asked to pay a fee to be offered a job or a work visa.
