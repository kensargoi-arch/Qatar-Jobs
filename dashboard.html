Application Progress — 10 Steps to Employment

Step 1 — Application Submitted In Progress
Your application form has been submitted to FindAJob.qa. Our recruitment team will review your details within 1–3 business days.
Awaiting your submission
Step 2 — Documents Under Review Upcoming
Our team reviews your uploaded passport, CV, certificates, and supporting documents for completeness and accuracy.
Step 3 — Employer Review Upcoming
Your profile is shared with the employer or sponsoring company. They review your qualifications and experience for the role.
Step 4 — Interview Scheduled Upcoming
If selected by the employer, an interview is arranged — by phone, video call, or in person. You will be notified via WhatsApp and email.
Step 5 — Job Offer Issued Upcoming
Congratulations! A formal job offer and employment contract are issued. Review carefully before signing. The employer covers all visa fees.
Step 6 — Visa Processing Upcoming
Your employer / sponsor submits the Qatar work visa application to the Ministry of Interior on your behalf. You may need to provide additional documents at this stage.
Step 7 — Visa Approved Upcoming
Your Qatar work visa is approved. Your entry permit will be shared with you. Ensure your passport has at least 6 months of validity remaining.
Step 8 — Flight Preparation Upcoming
Your employer or agency arranges your flight to Qatar. Carry original documents: passport, job offer letter, medical certificate, and police clearance.
Step 9 — Arrival in Qatar Upcoming
You arrive in Qatar. Your employer collects you from the airport. You undergo biometric registration and medical checks if not already completed.
Step 10 — Employment Started Upcoming
You begin work. Your Qatar ID (Residence Permit) is issued within a few weeks. Welcome to Qatar — your new career starts here!

Qatar Work Visa — Basic Requirements

Valid passport (minimum 6 months validity)
Confirmed job offer from Qatari employer
Employer sponsorship (kafala system)
Signed employment contract
Recent passport-size photographs
Educational certificates (if role requires)
Police clearance certificate
Medical examination (GAMCA-approved)
Biometric registration on arrival
Qatar ID issued after arrival & approval
Important: Qatar operates an employer-sponsored (kafala) visa system. Your employer applies for your visa on your behalf. You cannot independently obtain a standard work visa. Never pay any fee for a visa or job offer — this is illegal under Qatar labour law.
